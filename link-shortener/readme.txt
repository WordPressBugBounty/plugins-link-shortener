Link Shortner allows you to easily create clean, branded short links for your posts custom URL.

== Description ==

Link Shortner helps you generate short links and share any URL on or off your WordPress website.  
With this plugin, you can:

* Create short links for posts, pages, or custom URLs.
* Use your own domain name for better branding.
* Share short links on social media, emails, or anywhere online.
* Quickly regenerate links with one click from your WordPress admin.

Whether you’re managing content, marketing campaigns, or just want neat and professional URLs, Link Shortner makes it simple and effective.

== Installation ==

1. From your WordPress dashboard, go to **Plugins → Add New**.  
   Search for **Link Shortner**, then click **Install** and **Activate**.  

2. Or upload manually:  
   Upload the `link-shortner` folder to the `/wp-content/plugins/` directory and activate it from the **Plugins** menu in WordPress.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= How do I generate a short link? =  
After installing Link Shortner, go to the **Posts** section.  
There you’ll see a **Regenerate Link** button that creates a short URL for your post.
